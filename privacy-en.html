Privacy Policy for the Provodnik Application

Effective date: August 31, 2026 Current Version ID: ru.provodnik.app

This Policy describes how information is handled when users access the Provodnik mobile application (Android application ID ru.provodnik.app, the “Application”).

1. Controller

Controller Details
  • Data Controller & Owner: self-employed individual Макеева Софья Алексеевна
  • Location: Oryol, Oryol Region, Russian Federation
  • Public developer name: “Provodnik”
  • Privacy contact: provodnik.app@ro.ru

2. Age

The Application is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Where local law requires parental or guardian authorization for a minor, the Application may be used only with that authorization.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. A parent or guardian may request deletion of information submitted in error by contacting us.

3. Information we handle

3.1 Data stored locally

A display name, settings, learning progress, achievements, activity statistics, the educational virtual file system, and virtual game indicators may be stored solely on the user's device. This information is not synchronized with our servers. The Application does not provide registration or cloud accounts. Users may remove local information through the Application where available, by clearing Application storage, or by uninstalling it.

3.2 Local technical error reports

When an error occurs, the Application may create a minimized technical report and store it only on the user's device. The report contains the UTC event time, Application version, platform type, error type without the error message, and up to 20 stack-trace lines relating only to Application code.

The report does not include the user's name, email address, profile name, learning progress, file or text-field contents, advertising identifier, IP address, or full exception message. It is not transmitted automatically to us or any third party.

A user may select “Report an error” to open an external email client with the prepared report. The user can inspect, modify, or remove any information before choosing whether to send the email.

3.3 Yandex Mobile Ads

The Application uses Yandex Mobile Ads for rewarded and interstitial advertising. Before the SDK is initialized manually, the user chooses personalized or basic mode. In personalized mode, the SDK receives an affirmative consent signal. In basic mode, it receives a negative consent signal and must restrict processing according to SDK rules and applicable law. Depending on SDK settings, device settings, region, and user choice, Yandex and its advertising partners may process advertising or technical identifiers, IP address and network information, device and OS characteristics, Application information, ad interactions, diagnostics, fraud-prevention data, frequency-capping data, and advertising measurement data.

We do not intentionally provide precise location, name, email address, phone number, payment information, or learning-profile content in an ad request, and SDK location tracking is disabled. Users can withdraw consent and opt out of personalized advertising in Application settings. Basic non-personalized mode is used without consent; where that mode is not lawful, the advertising SDK must remain disabled.

3.4 Email support

The feedback control opens the user's external email client and does not send a message automatically. If a user voluntarily contacts us, we may process the sender's email address and name, message contents, attachments, message timestamps and headers, and a minimized technical error report if the user chooses to include it after reviewing its contents.

Users should not send passwords, payment credentials, identity documents, health information, or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained until the request is resolved and then for no longer than three years unless a longer period is legally necessary.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Information may be processed to provide Application functionality, preserve local progress, diagnose errors, respond to support requests, serve and measure advertising, prevent fraud, comply with law, and protect users and the controller.

Depending on the jurisdiction, legal bases may include consent, performance of the user agreement, steps requested by the user, legitimate interests in security and reliability, and compliance with legal obligations. Personalized advertising requires a separate choice where applicable. Refusal must not block the core educational functionality.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Information may be processed by:

  • Yandex group companies and advertising partners, for ad delivery, measurement, and security;
  • email providers, for delivery and storage of voluntarily submitted requests;
  • public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

We do not sell personal data. International transfers are made only where a lawful basis and required safeguards are available. Services that cannot lawfully process information in a user's jurisdiction must be disabled there.

6. Retention

  • Local progress: until removed by the user or the Application is uninstalled;
  • Local technical error report: until replaced by a later report, Application storage is cleared, or the Application is uninstalled;
  • Support email and voluntarily submitted error reports: until resolution and then up to three years unless law requires otherwise;
  • Advertising data: according to the provider's applicable retention rules and law.

Information is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed, unless retention is required by law.

7. User rights

Depending on location, users may request access, correction, restriction, deletion, portability, withdrawal of consent, or objection to processing, and may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. Consent can be withdrawn without affecting prior lawful processing.

Requests may be sent to provodnik.app@ro.ru. We may request information necessary to verify identity and locate the relevant correspondence.

8. Security

We use reasonable organizational and technical measures, data minimization, access controls, and encrypted transmission. No transmission or storage method is completely secure.

9. Third-party services

Third-party processing is also governed by the applicable provider terms and privacy notices. Users should review them before enabling optional services. We do not control external websites opened at the user's request.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy when the Application, law, or service providers change. The current version will be published at the same permanent public URL with a revised effective date. A new consent will be requested where legally required.

11. Contact

Privacy requests and inquiries: provodnik.app@ro.ru.
